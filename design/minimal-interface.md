# Minimal interface · September 14, 2026

Home contains an automatically focused search field and the Continuity, Top trail, and Return share rings. No greeting, cards, weekly chart, or explanatory copy competes with them. Enter searches locally; Escape clears the search. Each ring opens its calculation, coverage, comparison, and source pages.

The user's brief takes precedence over the attached Modal reference. We use its dark canvas, pale phosphor text, restrained green accent, hairline borders, and negative space. The rounded navigation capsule follows the user's requested geometry; the reference's marketing hero, top navigation, and illustrations do not apply.

The desktop sidebar is centered vertically at the left. At phone widths it becomes a centered bottom dock. Named tooltips and accessible labels identify icon controls. Search and rings remain in the same order at every size. Light mode uses a warm pale surface and the same hierarchy.

Your trails retains pinning, personal names and notes, continuation, optional sessions, recent pages, and record details. Graph, Explore, Settings and evidence share the new neutral palette. Rings use identical muted ink for every comparison direction; green identifies navigation and focus, not performance.

Validation: all 40 unit/protocol/DOM test files; Studio browser suite (six screens, two themes, five widths); full trail/evidence/session/persistence browser suite.
