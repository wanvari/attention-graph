# Cognitive Trails v5 — current runtime check

Recorded September 5, 2026. Apple M3 Pro (Mac15,6), 18 GiB unified memory. Local Ollama; bge-m3:latest embeddings and qwen3:4b labels. Synthetic browsing only. No personal history was read for this evaluation.

## Method

The committed fixture contains 384 candidate pages over a 28-day schedule. Only visited, eligible pages enter analysis: 410 visit records and 224 page aggregates in this run. This is not a claim that all 384 candidate pages were processed. The vector cache starts empty, both models are unloaded before the run, calls are sequential, and the production profile uses 50% pacing, an 8192-token chat context, an 1800-token output cap, and at most 100 pending pages per pass. The same stored record is processed repeatedly until its classification backlog is empty.

Complete prompts and responses, fresh vectors, and the metadata snapshot are committed under fixtures/current. The current protocol test replays exact prompt hashes and asserts final topic identities, labels, memberships, lifecycle state, and an empty classification backlog. Archived 12b transcripts remain separate.

## Observed performance

Pass 1: 79.33 seconds, including 18.87 seconds for embeddings and 60.24 seconds for labels. 100 pages embedded; 122 pending after commit.

Pass 2: 47.37 seconds, including 17.57 seconds for embeddings and 29.64 seconds for labels. 95 pages embedded, five vectors reused; 22 pending after commit.

Pass 3: 5.44 seconds, including 5.35 seconds for embeddings. 22 pages embedded; zero pending after commit.

Total: 132.14 seconds across three bounded passes, including about 65.64 seconds of deliberate pacing. All passes succeeded. Scheduled passes normally occur on later hourly idle checks; the evaluation runs them consecutively to measure processing cost.

Peak reported residency for the two configured models was 3,856,589,127 bytes (3.59 GiB), sampled through Ollama /api/ps after inference calls. No overlap between the embedding and chat model was observed at those samples. This is model residency, not a continuous measurement of system memory, process RSS, battery use, or peak GPU utilization.

Four labeling calls used 229–1962 prompt tokens and 110–445 output tokens. Each ended normally with structured output. No ambiguity-check calls were required by this particular final run; the earlier exploratory run exercised that path, and deterministic tests cover the ambiguity outcomes and caps.

The Home record model took 9.47 ms to construct after loading the snapshot. That number excludes IndexedDB read latency and browser layout/paint. Browser tests separately check the rendered flows and horizontal overflow at 390, 768, and 1440 pixels.

## Grouping quality and limitations

All 200 eligible fixture pages with a clear ground-truth topic received a membership. Of 24 eligible ambiguous/junk pages, 11 also received a membership and 13 remained ungrouped. Mixed link roundups are the clearest remaining weakness. They can be grouped by publication or page format even when their underlying subjects differ.

Among 1730 within-trail page pairs, 1697 joined the same clear ground-truth topic: pair precision 98.09%. Ambiguous/junk pairs are treated as incorrect rather than given a shared valid topic. This number alone overstates the experience on mixed pages; read it with the 11-of-24 noise assignment result above. It is not a human judgment of label quality, a calibrated probability, or evidence of cognition/intent detection.

The Home snapshot contains 415 reconciled observations across 228 distinct identities, including five capture observations not reconciled to imported history. Its observation denominator deliberately differs from the pipeline's 410 imported visits. Capture-only records stay available for retrieval; they do not imply completed classification. Interaction timing in this fixture is seeded test data, not a measurement of a participant.

No false revival was emitted while draining this backlog after the lifecycle fix. Earlier processing had exposed that error; a dedicated regression now ensures that old evidence cannot manufacture a return.

## Readiness

Unit, protocol, migration, copy, and DOM checks cover failure recovery and the current transcript. The actual Chrome capture suite checks forms, sensitive URLs, SPA navigation, conversation refresh, pause, and deletion. Chrome pipeline checks cover real Ollama, offscreen shutdown, abandoned runs, and duplicate scheduling. The product flow checks local search, notes, pinning, persistence after reload, settings, and pause through the actual worker.

This is evidence for a local pilot on one M3 Pro, not a performance guarantee for all laptops. Further work should measure mixed-page grouping with independent material, real retrieval success, mislabeled trails, long-history load time, battery use, and smaller-memory/non-Apple machines. Agreeing model outputs are a consistency signal, not scientific validation.
